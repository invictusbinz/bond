Product Concept / Working Document
A relational
operating system.
An AI-native space to understand yourself, navigate conflict, and grow across every relationship that matters to you.
Status Early Concept
Audience Yash (hey 👋)
Stage Product Discovery
Reading Time 6 to 8 mins

This idea came from something real. We've been together 10 years, we both want to grow, individually and together, and we fight. Sometimes well, sometimes not. We already use async convos as a deliberate practice for this, and it genuinely helps us communicate and feel heard. But the platform we use for it is just a group chat. No memory, no structure, no intelligence. It can't help us articulate what we actually mean, or make sure the other person has truly understood before they respond.

This is the intelligent version of that. Built for people who already want to do the work and just need the right scaffold to do it well.

The Insight
The enemy isn't conflict.
It's the conditions around it.

Most people in committed relationships aren't bad communicators. They're rational, caring people who get hijacked by emotion, timing, and format in the middle of a hard moment. The fight they have is rarely the fight they intended to have.

Current tools don't help. Therapy is expensive, periodic, and one-sided. Couples counseling needs both people in a room at the same time. Journaling has no intelligence. AI companions have no relationship context.

The gap is enormous. And it's hiding in plain sight.

"Being heard isn't the path to resolution. It's the destination, most of the time."

The product's primary job is witnessed understanding: not verdict, not judgment, not scoring. Resolution is a secondary mode that only becomes possible after both people feel genuinely seen.

Product Architecture
Two layers.
One coherent whole.

The product has a personal layer (just you) and a relational layer (you + the people in your life). Both are connected. The AI learns across all of them simultaneously.

◎
Personal Layer
The Self
Your private home base. The AI here knows you across all your relationships: your communication patterns, recurring blindspots, growth arc over time. You can process thoughts, set intentions (no pressure, they emerge naturally), and track how you're evolving as a person. The AI coaches you as an individual, not just within each relationship.
⌀
Relational Layer
Bonds
Each important relationship in your life gets its own Bond: your partner, your sister, a close friend. Each Bond has its own memory, its own history, and its own shared and private spaces. The other person may or may not participate. The Bond is useful either way.
Inside a Bond
Everything a relationship
needs to grow.
01
Sessions: structured conflict work
When there's something to work through together. Both people engage with the shared AI agent independently, on their own time and device. The AI holds everything: it synthesizes both sides neutrally, asks private follow-up questions to each person, and guides the conversation toward mutual understanding. No raw inputs are ever shown to the other party. Only the AI's synthesis is shared. This is non-negotiable. People self-censor when they know their partner will read their raw words verbatim.
Async-first Both parties AI synthesis only
02
Dispatches: solo processing
You don't always need the other person involved. A Dispatch is your private space within a Bond to think out loud, process a fight alone, make a note, or just clear your head. The AI already has full Bond context. It knows your history with this person, so it can respond intelligently even without them in the room. Some Dispatches stay private. Others can be moved to the shared Archive when you're ready.
Solo Private by default Full Bond context
03
The Foundation: shared agreements & goals
A living document co-created by both people. Communication agreements you've made, principles that guide how you fight, goals you share as a unit. The AI actively references this during Sessions and can gently flag when a principle is being violated without making it punitive. Just a mirror.
Shared Proactive
04
The Archive: memory and wins
Two flavors: a shared Archive (good memories, milestones, wins that belong to both of you, the stuff that reminds you why you're doing this) and a private Archive (notes only you can see, things you want to remember about this relationship but not necessarily share).
Shared version Private version
05
The Debrief: individual coaching post-session
After every Session, each person privately gets a coaching reflection. What you did well. Where your approach created distance. Patterns the AI is noticing over time. Specific, concrete things to practice. The goal of this feature is to eventually make the acute tool (Sessions) needed less often, not more. The product succeeds when you get better at this without it.
Private Growth-oriented
06
The Thread: longitudinal memory
The AI remembers everything across every Session and Dispatch within a Bond. Three months later, when a similar fight surfaces, it recognizes the pattern. It can say "this feels familiar. Here's what happened last time, here's what worked, here's what didn't." The relationship has a memory that neither person has to maintain manually.
Persistent Auto-built
How a Session Works
The core loop.

A Session has two modes, declared at the start by whoever initiates.

Mode 1 / Default
I need to be heard
The goal is witnessed understanding. No problem-solving. No action items. Success = the other person reflects back what you meant, not just what you said. This is most conflicts, most of the time.
Mode 2 / Optional
We need to figure something out
Resolution is on the table. Multi-round structured exchange, shared mapping of the disagreement, actionable next steps. Only possible after Mode 1 completes, or when both people are already calm enough to problem-solve.
1
Emotional availability check-in
Before the other person even reads anything, the AI asks them: "How are you showing up right now?" If they're depleted or defensive, it doesn't block them, but it primes them with what kind of energy this needs. "This is emotion-first. Your partner needs to feel heard, not solved."
2
Independent intake, both parties
Each person shares their side privately with the AI, in their own time, on their own device. No character limits. No formatting required. The AI asks gentle follow-up questions to help them articulate what they actually mean, going beyond what they literally said.
3
AI synthesis, shared and neutral
The AI synthesizes both sides into a shared view. Not a summary of what each person said. A distillation of what each person meant. Both people see this together. Raw inputs stay private permanently.
4
Rounds of clarification
Multiple rounds of back-and-forth, each person with the AI independently. The AI builds on everything shared so far and presents each party's contributions in plain, simple terms as the picture gets clearer. Direct questions between parties are possible, passed through the AI to preserve emotional temperature.
5
The checkpoint
"Both of you have acknowledged each other's feelings. Do you want to leave it here, or work toward figuring something out together?" This question, arriving at the right moment, makes the transition to resolution feel like a joint choice. Not something one person imposes on the other.
6
The Debrief, individual and private
Each person gets their private coaching reflection after the session closes. The Thread updates automatically. The Foundation is referenced if any shared agreements were relevant or broken.
Design Principles
What we refuse to compromise on.
Async-native
Both people can't always be on the same bandwidth. The tool is designed for emotional availability, not calendar availability.
Opinionated defaults
The tool has a point of view about healthy communication. It embodies it by default, without preaching it. Minimal settings, maximum intelligence.
Privacy as architecture
Raw inputs are never shared. The firewall between private and shared spaces is absolute. Trust is the product.
Anti-handicap
The tool should make itself needed less over time, not more. Growth, not dependency. The coaching layer exists to build skill, not replace it.
Heard before solved
Understanding always precedes resolution. The tool never skips the first to rush to the second.
No intimidation
No goals required to start. No forms to fill. The desire to grow is enough. Everything else, patterns, intentions, goals, emerges through use.
North Star
"The product succeeds when you need the fight resolution feature less, because you've gotten better at this without it."
Usage of the acute tool going down while engagement with the coaching layer goes up. That's the health metric, not daily active users on the conflict feature. This is a product that wins by making part of itself redundant.
Status & Next Steps
Where this is right now.

This document represents the product philosophy and full vision, not an MVP spec. The concept is directionally clear. The next step is to define the smallest version of this that is still true to the vision and genuinely useful on day one.

Key open questions worth discussing: the onboarding moment (how does a second person get invited into a Bond?), the AI's voice and tone, and where to draw the MVP boundary without betraying the core idea.
